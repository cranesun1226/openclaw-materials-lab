# Perovskite Solar Materials Exploration Demo

## Research Goal

Can we identify a stable perovskite or perovskite-adjacent solar absorber candidate with a band gap near 1.1-2.2 eV using the current Materials Lab OpenClaw plugin?

## Evaluation Criteria

- Prefer low energy above hull as a stability proxy.
- Prefer band gap close to 1.5 eV for single-junction solar absorption.
- Use density only as a weak tie-breaker in this demo.
- Treat offline/mock results as workflow validation, not live materials discovery.
